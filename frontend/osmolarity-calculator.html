Frequently Asked Questions

What is osmolarity?
Osmolarity is the measure of solute concentration expressed as osmoles of solute per litre of solution (osmol/L or mOsm/L).
How is osmolarity calculated?
Osmolarity = Σ(concentration × number of particles). For non-electrolytes like glucose, particles = 1. For NaCl, particles = 2 (Na⁺ and Cl⁻).
What is the difference between osmolarity and osmolality?
Osmolarity is per litre of solution; osmolality is per kilogram of solvent. They are approximately equal for dilute aqueous solutions.
What is isotonic?
Isotonic solutions have approximately the same osmolarity as blood plasma (~285-310 mOsm/L).
