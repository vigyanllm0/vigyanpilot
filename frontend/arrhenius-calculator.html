Frequently Asked Questions

What is t90?
Time required for 10% degradation (90% potency remaining). First-order: t90 = −ln(0.9)/k.
What is activation energy?
Energy barrier for degradation (J/mol). Higher Ea = stronger temperature dependence of degradation rate.
Why does shelf-life drop with temperature?
Per Arrhenius, rate k increases exponentially with temperature. Higher temperature → faster degradation → shorter shelf-life.
