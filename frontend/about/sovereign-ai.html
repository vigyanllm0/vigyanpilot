What Sovereign AI Means for Research Data

Sovereign AI means the institution retains control over its data, compute, and models. For life sciences, where genomic sequence data, patient-linked research datasets, and clinical diagnostics information carry strict regulatory obligations, data sovereignty is not a convenience — it is a compliance requirement. A sovereign AI platform keeps data residency under the researcher's authority, avoids data egress to third-country servers, and removes dependence on foreign cloud providers for critical research workflows.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023 establishes a framework in which sensitive personal data, including health and genomic data, must be processed lawfully and with explicit consent. VigyanLLM's architecture is designed around DPDP-considerate handling: on-premise execution means sensitive sequences never leave your infrastructure, and consent-based processing is respected at every stage.

On-Premise Deployment for Full Digital Sovereignty

Full digital sovereignty comes from owning the infrastructure. VigyanLLM deploys via Docker Compose on Ubuntu 22.04 LTS with optional CUDA GPU acceleration, enabling an air-gapped or self-hosted installation inside your institutional network. Once deployed, all computation runs locally — primer thermodynamics, BLAST specificity screening, and docking engines execute on your hardware with zero external API calls and zero per-query costs.

  • Air-gapped capable: no internet connection required after initial deployment.
  • Zero data egress: sequence data, primers, and reports never leave your network.
  • LDAP integration: authenticate with institutional directory services.
  • Unlimited internal runs: no per-query metering on your own compute.

This makes VigyanLLM well suited to a sovereign cloud strategy, where Indian institutions and government research bodies can run sensitive life sciences workloads on domestic infrastructure under their direct control.

Frequently Asked Questions: Sovereign AI India

What is sovereign AI and why does it matter for Indian labs?

Sovereign AI is AI where data, compute, and control remain with the deploying institution or country rather than a foreign provider. For Indian research labs it matters because genomic and health data fall under the DPDP Act 2023, and keeping that data on Indian or self-managed infrastructure avoids compliance risk and third-country data transfer concerns.

Is VigyanLLM truly Make in India?

Yes. VigyanLLM is designed, developed, and operated by an Indian team in Gurgaon, Haryana. The product, pipelines, and documentation are built domestically, and the platform supports INR pricing, GST invoicing, and domestic payment methods through Razorpay.

Does VigyanLLM send my data outside India?

No. In the on-premise deployment, all sequence data and computation stay within your institution's infrastructure with zero external API calls. The public web version serves tools directly; sensitive institutional workloads are intended for self-hosted or air-gapped deployment under your control.

Is VigyanLLM DPDP Act compliant?

VigyanLLM is designed to be DPDP-considerate: the platform processes data with consent, supports data portability, and — in on-premise mode — keeps sensitive data within your jurisdiction. For formal compliance certifications applicable to your specific use, consult our team.

Can government institutions deploy VigyanLLM on-premise?

Yes. VigyanLLM deploys via Docker Compose on Ubuntu 22.04 LTS, is air-gapped capable, supports LDAP for institutional authentication, and offers PO-based billing and net-30 terms for government and public research bodies.

What does on-premise deployment cost for institutions?

On-premise licensing is available under our enterprise (Lab and above) plans with custom SLAs and support. Contact us at contact@vigyanllm.in for institutional pricing and deployment guidance.
