Understanding MPC

TermDefinition
MPCLowest concentration preventing growth of the least susceptible single-step mutant (zero colonies).
MICLowest concentration with ≤1% survival (visible growth inhibition).
MPC/MICRatio indicating the mutant selection window. Lower = lower resistance risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MPC?
The Mutant Prevention Concentration is the lowest antibiotic concentration that prevents growth of the least susceptible single-step mutant.
What is MPC/MIC ratio?
A low MPC/MIC ratio suggests a narrower mutant selection window and lower resistance risk.
How is MPC different from MIC?
MIC inhibits visible growth of the bulk population. MPC prevents growth of resistant sub-populations (single-step mutants), so MPC ≥ MIC.
