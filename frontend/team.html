Our Approach

Engineering & Science

Technical leadership for primer thermodynamics, BLAST integration, docking scoring, and multi-agent validation is provided internally by the engineering and molecular-biology team. Names and bios for individual contributors are being added as our public documentation program ramps up.

Review Process

Before any tool recommendation ships, a technical reviewer checks the underlying parameters (nearest-neighbour thermodynamics, salt and magnesium corrections, BLAST specificity) against peer-reviewed sources. This process is what lets us stand behind the claims on our pages. For the specific methods and citations, see the About page and the tool pages.
