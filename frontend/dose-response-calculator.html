Understanding 4PL Parameters

ParameterMeaning
BottomResponse at zero dose (asymptotic minimum)
TopMaximal response (asymptotic maximum)
EC50 / IC50Concentration giving 50% of maximal effect
Hill SlopeSteepness of the curve (cooperativity)
R²Goodness of fit — closer to 1 is better

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 4PL curve?
The 4-parameter logistic model: y = bottom + (top-bottom)/(1+10^((logEC50-x)*hillSlope)). It describes sigmoidal dose-response relationships.
What is EC50?
The concentration giving 50% of the maximal effect. For inhibition curves this is often reported as IC50.
What does R² mean?
Coefficient of determination. Values close to 1 indicate a good curve fit. Values below 0.9 suggest poor fit.
How many data points do I need?
Minimum 4 points. For reliable fits, use 6-8 points spanning the full curve including top and bottom plateaus.
