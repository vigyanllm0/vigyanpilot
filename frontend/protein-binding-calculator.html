Frequently Asked Questions

What is protein binding?
The fraction of drug bound to plasma proteins. Only unbound (free) drug is pharmacologically active.
Why correct for protein binding?
Highly protein-bound drugs (e.g. >90%) have very low free fractions, so the total dose must be higher to achieve the same free concentration.
How is free concentration calculated?
Free = Total × (1 − bound fraction). Bound = Total × bound fraction.
