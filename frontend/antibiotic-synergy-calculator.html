Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FIC index?
Fractional Inhibitory Concentration index = FIC-A + FIC-B, where FIC-A = MIC of drug A in combination / MIC of drug A alone.
How to interpret FIC index?
≤0.5 Synergistic, >0.5–1 Additive, >1–2 Indifferent, >2 Antagonistic.
What is a checkerboard assay?
A method to test antibiotic synergy where two drugs are combined in a 2D dilution matrix and the MIC of each in combination is determined.
