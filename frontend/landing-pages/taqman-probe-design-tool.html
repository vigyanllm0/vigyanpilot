Frequently Asked Questions: TaqMan probe design tool

How do I design a TaqMan probe?

TaqMan probe design requires a probe with Tm 8-10°C higher than the primers, positioned between the forward and reverse primer binding sites, with no G at the 5' end (quenching efficiency), and fewer than 4 consecutive Gs in the sequence. VigyanLLM automates these constraints: it designs the probe after primer selection, ensures proper Tm relationships, checks for secondary structure between probe and primers, and validates the complete assay for qPCR efficiency.
