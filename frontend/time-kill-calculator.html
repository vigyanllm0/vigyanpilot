Frequently Asked Questions

What is bactericidal?
Bactericidal = ≥3 log₁₀ reduction in CFU from initial count at 24 hours.
What is bacteriostatic?
Bacteriostatic = 1–3 log₁₀ reduction in CFU (growth inhibited but not killed).
What is time to 99.9% killing?
The time at which CFU drops to 0.1% (1/1000) of the initial count.
