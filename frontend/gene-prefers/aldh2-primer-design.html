ALDH2 Primer Design

PCR primer design guide for the ALDH2 (Aldehyde Dehydrogenase 2) gene located on chromosome 12q24. ALDH2 encodes the mitochondrial aldehyde dehydrogenase enzyme responsible for acetaldehyde oxidation in alcohol metabolism. Pre-configured parameters, SNP considerations, and optimized primer design for ALDH2 analysis.

About ALDH2

The ALDH2 gene (Aldehyde Dehydrogenase 2) on chromosome 12q24 The E504K variant (rs671) severely reduces ALDH2 activity and is carried by ~40% of East Asian populations, linking to cancer risk. Key targets include Exons 1–13 for comprehensive mutation screening.

Mutations: E504K (rs671), R475W | GC content: 61% | Target exons: Exons 1–13

ALDH2 Primer Design Challenges

  • High GC content (61%): The ALDH2 gene has elevated overall GC content requiring denaturant optimization for robust amplification
  • rs671 hotspot: The common E504K variant in exon 12 falls within primer binding regions requiring careful placement
  • Homologous ALDH genes: ALDH1A1, ALDH1B1 paralogs share 65–70% sequence identity necessitating BLAST-based primer validation

When designing ALDH2 primers, avoid these clinically significant variants:
