PCR primer design guide for the ATM (ATM Serine/Threonine Kinase) gene located on chromosome 11q22. About ATM

The ATM gene (ATM Serine/Threonine Kinase) on chromosome 11q22 Biallelic ATM mutations cause ataxia-telangiectasia, while monoallelic loss increases breast and pancreatic cancer risk. Key targets include Exons 2–63 (PI3K-like kinase domain in exons 39–63) for comprehensive mutation screening.

Mutations: Truncating, missense mutations | GC content: 44% | Target exons: Exons 2–63 (PI3K-like kinase domain in exons 39–63)

ATM Primer Design Challenges

  • Very large gene (62 exons): ATM spans ~150 kb with 62 coding exons requiring extensive tiled primer panels
  • Low GC content (44%): AT-rich regions near intron 8 affect primer annealing, requiring higher Tm primers
  • High SNP density: The large coding region contains numerous benign polymorphisms requiring frequent primer repositioning
