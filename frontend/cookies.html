Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

VigyanLLM is a sovereign biomedical AI platform. We minimise data collection and use analytics only with your consent. This policy explains the storage mechanisms used by our platform, how Google Analytics behaves, and your choices.

What We Use and Why

The VigyanLLM Primer tool uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage for essential application functions. Analytics via Google Analytics 4 is enabled only after you click Accept all on the consent banner; it remains disabled if you decline.

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may set their own cookies or storage when you interact with their components:

  • Google Analytics 4 (G-PB0XMF4GEH) — loads only after you click Accept all. We use Google Consent Mode v2; for visitors in the EEA/UK/Switzerland analytics stays disabled until explicit consent, while it is enabled by default for other regions until you decline. It measures aggregate page usage and does not feed personalised advertising.
  • Google OAuth 2.0 — when you sign in with Google, Google sets authentication cookies governed by Google's privacy policy
  • Razorpay — when you complete a payment, Razorpay's hosted checkout may set session cookies necessary for payment processing
  • Google Fonts — our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts; Google may log requests per its privacy policy

We do not control these third-party cookies. Please refer to the respective service's privacy policy for details.

Your Choices

When the consent banner appears, choose Accept all to enable Google Analytics or Decline optional to keep it disabled. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland analytics is off by default until you accept; in all other regions it is on by default until you decline. You can change your choice by clearing the vigyanllm_cookie_consent item in your browser's site data, which will show the banner again on your next visit.

You can clear localStorage and sessionStorage at any time through your browser settings (typically Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Site Data). Clearing this data will sign you out and remove your locally stored design history but will not affect your account on our servers.

Most browsers also provide private or incognito modes that clear sessionStorage automatically when you close the window.

Do Not Track

Our platform does not respond to Do Not Track (DNT) signals. Analytics behaviour is instead governed by your explicit Accept/Decline choice on the consent banner.

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or our data practices, contact us at contact@vigyanllm.in.
